1. EXPERIMENTAL CONTEXT & ROOT CAUSE OF EARLY 4% SOH:
- Total discharge cycles recorded in Battery_23: 67 cycles.
- Cycles 1 to 42 (NASA cycle_index 1 to 100): The test protocol applied short micro-cycles
  with discharge duration averaging 96 to 1060 seconds (mean current -0.25A to -1.77A).
  The cell voltage hit the 1.9V cutoff almost immediately because of cold temperature
  high internal impedance. Total delivered capacity was only ~0.045 to 0.056 Ah.
- Cycle 43 (NASA cycle_index 104): The chamber protocol was updated to a full continuous discharge.
  Discharge duration jumped to 5,608 seconds, delivering 1.2156 Ah.
- Because 1.2156 Ah was the first cycle >= 1.0 Ah, C_ref was correctly assigned at Cycle 43.
- Dividing early micro-cycle capacities (0.055 Ah) by C_ref (1.2156 Ah) yields exactly SOH = 4.5%!
